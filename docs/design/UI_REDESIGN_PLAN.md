# GrantPath — Evidence Desk

**Phase 1 design plan · October 25, 2026 · proposed, not implemented.**

Selected direction: a precise research workbench organized around **the next decision and the exact material behind it**. A persistent application/cycle context, readable working surface and on-demand source folio make the existing evidence/version mechanism visible. The signature interaction is a **decision trace**: inspect one concern's retained quotation, action, supporting evidence, pinned target revision, response and human decision; then see why a changed dependency needs review. Missing work stays missing.

This plan does not establish application acceptance or frontend authorization. The demo is expected October 26; its exact cutoff and duration are unknown. Prefer a complete, reliable source-to-decision demonstration and reserve time for integration and rehearsal.

## 2. Three composition studies and the choice

See [composition studies](ui-exploration/01-compositions.svg). All diagrams use illustrative synthetic labels and are static planning assets, not product screenshots or generated results.

| Direction | Spatial idea and researcher question | Advantages | Why it is not the whole-product choice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Annotated dossier** | A broad numbered folio; a document outline at left and citation footnotes/margin at right. “What exactly am I saying, and where is it supported?” | Excellent long narrative/quotation reading, editorial hierarchy, clear historical folio identity. | Repeated small state transitions and dense ledger/import reconciliation would become annotations on a very long document. Keep its reading typography and retained-source folio. |
| **B. Evidence Desk — selected** | A narrow workflow rail, compact context band, a work index that reuses available space, one active decision, optional adjacent exact-source folio. “What needs my decision next, and what changed underneath it?” | Makes a real trace and missing dependency legible early. Works for authoring, revision, terms and transactions without pretending their states are interchangeable. | Needs strict width discipline and buffer ownership. The evidence folio is on demand; a fourth permanent column is forbidden. |
| **C. Funding register** | Full-width aligned rows with period/version/source columns and an expandable detail strip; original/current comparison lives in columns. “Which recorded number or obligation changed, and what is its authority?” | Fast financial scan, excellent exact numeric comparison and source reconciliation; strong ledger character. | Poor default for long prose and scientific uncertainty. Use its table grammar inside finance/report/discovery surfaces, not as a universal product model. |

**Choose B**, borrowing A's readable folio and C's aligned register only where the task calls for them. The main researcher decision is whether a response/claim is supported by the selected versions and what must happen next. A ledger-first opening overemphasizes a later workflow; a narrative-only dossier buries the changed dependency. Evidence Desk can expose this mechanism within the first laptop viewport while preserving the rest of the application.

Its identity comes from a dark ink index, cobalt selected lines, a generous white working page, bracketed source excerpts and an amber gap rule. No decorative score tiles, fake counters, graph canvas, ornamental gradients, animated backgrounds or generic AI promises. Stage, source authority and human review are distinct text, not a stack of success badges.

Standard input/button minimum height **44 px**, register rows at least 44 when actionable. Persistent context band: **Project → application/call → selected cycle → current working/historical scope**, save status and one contextual action. Stage is user-recorded and editable separately; “Submitted” always retains its local-status explanation. Do not manufacture a linear progress percentage.

Global navigation proposed: **Project & sources; Opportunities; Application plan; Proposal; Revision & review; Award & progress; Finance & reports**. Only mount entries actually accepted on final main. Award and finance get a direct lifecycle home instead of a third nested drafting mode. Reminders appear beside relevant agenda items and a compact open-site banner, not as a new destination unless final code needs one. Reusable content remains a proposal utility; exports/history remain in their originating workflow.

Keep conventional links, buttons, selects, checkboxes, details and forms. The trace's selected item is a button; its actual relationships are a labeled list/definition structure. No drag-only edges or custom canvas keyboard model. Inspecting is read-only; “Use this exact version/passage” remains a distinct explicit mutation through the real command. Never reinterpret view selection as source acceptance.

Focus: 3px cobalt outline, 3px offset, two-tone light separation when on the ink rail; no focus removal. Use skip link, landmarks, logical headings, visible labels, associated hints/errors, live save/error status and announced count changes only when useful. Following a trace puts focus on the selected folio heading; Close returns it to the invoking link. Sheet/dialog traps focus only while actually modal; Escape closes inspection, never discards text. Tab order follows context → work index → decision → inspector. Do not let sticky content obscure focus at 200% zoom.

Motion: optional 120–160ms opacity/short translation for inspector reveal and a brief selected-line transition that explains context. No parallax, auto-scroll carousel or endless loading pulse. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes translation/smooth scrolling and nonessential transitions. Save confirmation is text; no animation required to understand it.

## 4. Signature interaction: inspect the decision trace

See [storyboard](ui-exploration/03-decision-trace.svg). It is a plan using synthetic illustrative labels, not a new fixture or a saved product result. Final demonstration selects actual existing isolated data after phase-2 inventory.

### Persisted relationships that justify it

`lib/resubmissions/schema.ts` and `matrix.ts` already retain concern citations/mappings, shared action `concernIds`, evidence citations/classification, target revision links, response revisions and human completion fingerprints. `context.ts` validates exact quotations/hash/ranges and revision digests. `view.ts` returns derived current issues and state after the view route hydrates current vault integrity. `comparison-view.ts` preserves historical mappings when target sections are archived. `components/common.tsx` `CitationView` follows `fileOrigin` to the exact retained original.

The trace is a **projection of these records**, not a new graph, source of truth or guessed universal contract. Feedback quotations and scientific/project evidence are separate namespaces. An action may address several concerns; evidence and target revision are independent branches under that action, not a false sequential claim that the source generated a revision. Requirement mapping appears only where a real mapping exists; a visually empty slot is not an inferred edge. Ordinary proposal citations attach to a **section revision**, not arbitrary sentence-level claim spans. Exact target spans exist in resubmission `RevisionLink`; do not invent a universal sentence-to-evidence graph or imply that a recorded link establishes scientific entailment.

### Six beats

1. **Choose a decision.** Revision & review opens a compact concern list with actual state/reason. Select one concern. Show selected target cycle and attested original identity above the passage. Other concerns remain accessible without a page-length matrix.
2. **Read the retained concern.** Show exact reviewer quotation and its feedback version. Open “Inspect retained passage” in the source folio. Label the active scope “Pinned v2 used by this concern”; if a latest supplied v3 exists, offer a separate read-only comparison. No version select silently reanchors the concern.
3. **Follow the actual action.** Show its description, action kind, reviewed outcome classification, actual linked evidence and exact saved target section/revision. Two supporting branches are visible with text. **For new-data/new-analysis**, missing required observed-result support stays an amber gap with the next valid action; proposed work cannot close it. Other action kinds retain their actual evidence/review and reasoned-disposition rules.
4. **Read the response and human decision.** Response text, cited evidence, missing inputs and exact completion decision are adjacent. Saving/downloading a response never resolves it. A completion control becomes available only under actual `completionIssues` rules; explanatory issues remain visible.
5. **Explain a changed dependency.** When current derived state says Needs review, show the retained link and latest supplied/current target side by side. `concernDependencies`/fingerprint includes mapped/linked target section heads, source heads/integrity, project/call revisions, context/conflicts and scientific action fields; owner/due are deliberately excluded from `scientificAction`. Do not claim every unrelated edit is harmless: project/call changes can invalidate more broadly. If the server does not report the precise cause, label “Inputs differ from the recorded decision,” list observable pinned/current differences and ask for review rather than inventing causality.
6. **Keep history inspectable.** Re-review uses existing explicit commands; the former completion and frozen packet remain historical. Download saved internal Markdown/JSON from the retained packet. Currentness is separate from byte availability. Return to the active editor with unsaved text, cursor/selection and source scope intact.

The hero proof is the supported action beside an unfinished action, followed by a **targeted stale decision** and unchanged historical packet. A prepared 60–90 second walkthrough is a rehearsal target, not a measured human outcome or assumed allotted demo duration. If final main lacks a needed projection detail, use the narrower existing source/revision inspection and report the gap; do not add invented backend relationships.

## 6. State, buffer and truth rules

### Client state ownership: preserve, then improve composition

Current shell owns loaded `State`, selection, busy/error, editor registry and staged `latestState`. Components own local text, selections, previews, retry IDs and abort controllers. `useEditorDirty` registers **booleans, not recoverable text**. `useBuffer` in resubmissions protects dirty values from prop refresh; all mounted proposal editors protect local blocks/citations. Simply moving their boolean registry upward would not preserve their text after unmount.

Phase-2 first choice is to **retain existing editor owners/mounting while changing visibility and surrounding layout**. A later extracted buffer store must have an explicit complete identity (project/application/cycle/section or feature record and base revision), retain structured blocks, citations, source/target anchors, edited context and operation identity, and be verified before replacing mounting. Do not use workspace revision as a React key, remount everything on server refresh, or overwrite dirty state from a new selected record. Keep independent buffers independently dirty.

Ordinary local selection/inspector/view changes retain unsaved text; changing project/application with dirty work offers keep-and-switch once safe retention exists, save/reconcile, or explicit discard. Until then keep the current cancelable guard. Save/freeze/export actions must refer to saved inputs and clearly state when local edits are excluded; existing dirty restrictions stay until real contracts safely support a narrower scope. No broad “Save all” promise without actual multi-editor behavior.

409 rejects the **current request**. An earlier lost-response attempt may already have committed; existing exact-operation recovery returns that durable prior result before stale-revision rejection where supported. Keep local text/base/operation ID. Show saved server version side by side, let the researcher reconcile, then explicitly retry under the proper current heads. Distinguish **refresh/inspect saved reference — keep editor text** from **discard local edits / use saved**. Current shell `load(false)` and baseline-adoption controls, and 05's saved-data reload, preserve dirty editor text; a component buffer's explicit discard/reload can replace it. Label the actual action, never require or silently trigger discard merely to refresh reference data. Do not invent automatic text merge or rotate an operation key during a lost-response retry. Do not clear dirty state on failure. Cross-view retention is in-session; reload/closed-browser persistence of unsaved buffers is not promised without separately implemented storage.
